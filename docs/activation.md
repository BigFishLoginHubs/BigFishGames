# Big Fish Games Activation

Game activation can involve verifying a purchase, account, license, or other entitlement associated with a game.

The exact activation process can vary depending on the game, platform, and purchase method.

## Before Activating

Make sure that:

- You are using the correct account.
- You obtained the game from an authorized source.
- Your internet connection is working when online activation is required.
- The game is installed correctly.
- Your device meets the game's requirements.

## General Activation Process

A typical process may look like this:

1. Install the game.
2. Launch the game.
3. Sign in if prompted.
4. Follow the activation instructions shown by the application.
5. Complete any required verification.
6. Start the game after activation is completed.

## Activation Problems

Common activation problems may include:

- Activation fails
- The game cannot verify ownership
- The wrong account is being used
- An activation screen repeatedly appears
- The game cannot connect to the required service

## Troubleshooting Activation

Try the following:

1. Confirm that you are signed in to the correct account.
2. Check your internet connection.
3. Restart the game.
4. Restart your device.
5. Check whether an update is available.
6. Verify that the game was obtained through an authorized source.

If activation still fails, consult the official support resources.

!!! warning "Keep Activation Information Private"
    Do not publish private activation codes, license information, passwords, or account credentials online.
