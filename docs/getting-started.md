# Getting Started

This page provides general information for users who are getting started with Big Fish Games.

## Before You Begin

Before downloading or installing a game, consider checking:

- Your device or operating-system compatibility
- Available storage space
- Internet connectivity
- Your account information
- The game's system requirements
- The official source of the game

## Basic Process

A typical game setup process may involve:

1. Finding the desired game.
2. Checking compatibility and requirements.
3. Obtaining the game through an authorized source.
4. Downloading the required files.
5. Installing the game.
6. Launching the game.
7. Signing in or completing activation if required.

## Choosing a Download Source

For safety and reliability, use official or authorized sources whenever possible.

Avoid downloading game installers from unknown websites because files from untrusted sources may be modified, incomplete, or unsafe.

## Account Information

If a game requires an account, keep your login credentials private.

Never publish passwords, payment information, security answers, or private account information on a public website.

## If Something Goes Wrong

If you encounter a problem during setup:

- Check your internet connection.
- Restart the application.
- Restart your device.
- Check available storage.
- Verify compatibility.
- Check for updates.
- Review the relevant troubleshooting information.

If the issue involves an account, purchase, payment, or other private information, use official customer-support channels.
