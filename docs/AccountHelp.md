# Big Fish Games Account Help

Account problems can prevent users from accessing games, purchases, or other services.

## Common Account Problems

Users may experience:

- Login problems
- Forgotten passwords
- Account access problems
- Missing purchases
- Incorrect account information
- Difficulty accessing previously purchased games

## Login Problems

If you cannot sign in:

1. Confirm that you are using the correct email address.
2. Check that your password is entered correctly.
3. Use the official password-recovery process if available.
4. Check your internet connection.
5. Try signing in again later if the service is temporarily unavailable.

## Forgotten Password

Use the official account-recovery or password-reset process.

Do not give your password to another person claiming to provide support.

## Missing Purchases

If a purchase does not appear in your account:

- Confirm that you are using the correct account.
- Check your purchase history.
- Review your confirmation email if available.
- Keep relevant order information available.
- Contact official support if the purchase remains missing.

## Protect Your Account

Never publicly share:

- Passwords
- Payment-card details
- Security questions and answers
- Private activation information
- Account-recovery codes

## Account Support

For account-specific problems, use the official Big Fish Games support resources rather than posting sensitive information publicly.
